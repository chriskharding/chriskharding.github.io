What it is

Plasma, not laser. Sublimation, not burning.

Lasers and radiofrequency heat the tissue under your skin to force it to tighten. Plexr Plus works differently. It ionizes the nitrogen in the air into a tiny plasma arc that sublimates the outermost layer of skin at a single point — turning it directly to gas — without conducting heat into the layers below. The skin around each point contracts as it heals, and the result is tightening with far less collateral injury.

Because plasma acts on the surface rather than on pigment or water in the skin, it can be used across skin types, needs no general anesthesia, and leaves no incision. Plexr Plus is FDA-cleared for the removal and destruction of skin lesions and for tissue coagulation, and is supported by more than a decade of clinical use in Europe.

Common questions

Plexr Plus, answered.

What is Plexr Plus?

Plexr Plus is a medical plasma device made in Italy. It turns the nitrogen in the air into a small plasma arc that sublimates — converts to gas — the very top layer of skin at a single point, without cutting or burning the tissue underneath. The controlled micro-injury tightens the skin as it heals. It is FDA-cleared for the removal and destruction of skin lesions and for tissue coagulation.

Is a Plexr eyelid lift the same as surgery?

No. Surgical blepharoplasty removes skin with a scalpel under anesthesia. Plexr Plus tightens the existing skin with plasma under topical numbing, with no incision. Results are more modest than surgery and suit people with mild to moderate excess skin who want a natural change with a short recovery.

Does it hurt?

Topical numbing is applied first. Most people describe a warm, prickling sensation and rate the discomfort as mild. Eyelids are the most sensitive area and are numbed thoroughly.

What is the downtime?

Expect swelling for two to three days (more around the eyes) and small brown carbon crusts that fall off on their own in about 5–7 days. Most people return to desk work within a day or two. Makeup can be used once the crusts have shed. Sun protection matters for several weeks.

How many sessions will I need?

Skin tags, moles, and milia are usually a single visit. Eyelids, necks, and resurfacing often benefit from two sessions spaced six to eight weeks apart. Your clinician will tell you what to expect for your area at the consultation.

Who is it for?

Adults with mild to moderate skin laxity, benign skin lesions, surface pigmentation, or textured scarring who want a non-surgical option. Because plasma acts on the skin surface rather than on pigment, it can be used across skin types. It is not suitable during pregnancy, on active infection, or on lesions that have not been evaluated.
