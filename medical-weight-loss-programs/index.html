Common questions

Questions, answered plainly.

How much does it cost to get started?

The new-patient offer is $550 (regularly $750) and includes your provider consultation, metabolic evaluation, program design, and a $200 credit toward your program. If a GLP-1 medication is appropriate for you, it is prescribed after your evaluation and your credit can be applied to it. If a GLP-1 program isn’t the right tool, your visit is credited toward the program that fits. Ongoing pricing is walked through plainly at your first visit — no surprises.

Do you prescribe semaglutide or tirzepatide?

When appropriate, yes. GLP-1 medications are prescribed after your labs and consultation confirm they’re a fit for your health picture — and they’re always paired with nutrition coaching and provider supervision, because medication alone isn’t a program.

How much weight will I lose?

We don’t promise numbers — everyone responds differently. What the published research shows: in clinical trials, adults taking semaglutide lost about 15% of their body weight on average over 68 weeks (the STEP 1 trial), and adults taking tirzepatide lost up to roughly 21% on average over 72 weeks at the highest studied dose (the SURMOUNT-1 trial). Your results will differ — which is exactly why our program is built on your labs, supervised by your provider, and adjusted as your body responds. Individual results vary.

What makes this different from online GLP-1 services?

A provider who actually knows you. Comprehensive labs before you start, in-person support, body-composition tracking, and follow-up that adapts your program — not a subscription box with a questionnaire.

I keep hearing about newer weight loss medications in trials — can you help me get them?

Investigational medications are only legitimately available through registered clinical trials — never through pharmacies or online sellers, no matter what ads claim. The FDA has warned companies selling unapproved “compounded” versions of investigational drugs. If you’re curious about what’s coming, we’ll help you understand the published research and point you to legitimate trials at clinicaltrials.gov. And the moment a new medication earns FDA approval, we evaluate it for our program.
