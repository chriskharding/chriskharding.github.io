What does $350/month include?

Your membership is all-inclusive: comprehensive labs, unhurried visits, direct provider access, and ongoing plan management. Exactly what’s included is walked through plainly at your discovery call — no surprise bills.

How is this different from my regular doctor?

We’re not a replacement for emergencies or hospital care — we’re the ongoing, proactive layer most healthcare is missing. Longer visits, deeper labs, direct access, and a plan that’s actually managed over time.

Do you accept insurance?

Membership and services are direct-pay, which is what allows unhurried visits and direct access. We can provide a superbill for you to submit to your insurance for possible out-of-network reimbursement, and labs and prescriptions can often still run through your insurance. Membership is HSA/FSA eligible — we provide documentation. One important note: we have opted out of Medicare, so by law neither we nor you can submit claims to Medicare for our services.

Why is membership limited?

Because access is the product. We keep our patient panel intentionally small so every member gets time, attention, and a provider who actually knows them.
