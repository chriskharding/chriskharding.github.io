What it is

Cold laser. Fat cells empty, not destroyed.

Most body-contouring devices work by injuring fat — freezing it, heating it, or suctioning it out — and injury is where the pain and the downtime come from. Zerona does something quieter. Its low-level red laser passes through the skin and prompts fat cells to open temporary pores and release their stored contents. Your lymphatic system clears what's released, the cells shrink, and the area measures smaller.

Because nothing is damaged, there is nothing to recover from. No numbness, no bruising, no compression garments. The trade-off is honest: results are more modest than surgery, and they last as long as your weight does — which is why we treat Zerona as part of a program, not a shortcut around one.

Common questions

Zerona, answered.

What is Zerona?

Zerona is a low-level ("cold") laser. Its red light passes through the skin and prompts fat cells beneath it to release their stored contents, which your lymphatic system then clears. The cells shrink and the treated area measures smaller. Nothing is heated, frozen, cut, or injected, which is why there is no pain and no downtime. Zerona is FDA-cleared as a non-invasive aesthetic treatment for the reduction of body circumference.

Does it hurt? Is there downtime?

No. You feel nothing during treatment — the laser is cold. There is no redness, bruising, numbness, or recovery. People routinely come in on a lunch break and go straight back to work.

How much can I expect to lose?

Results are measured in inches of circumference, not pounds. In the manufacturer's clinical studies, participants lost an average of about 3.7 inches combined across the waist, hips, and thighs after one six-session course. Your result depends on your starting point and how well you support the process between sessions. Individual results vary, and we measure before and after so you know exactly what changed.

How many sessions do I need?

The FDA-cleared protocol is six sessions over two weeks, every other day. Some people choose a second course after a break for additional areas or further reduction; your clinician will advise.

Is it permanent?

Zerona shrinks fat cells rather than destroying them, so the result holds as long as your weight does. That is exactly why we pair it with a real program: many patients use Zerona to reshape the areas that remain after medical weight loss, and keep the result by keeping the habits.

How is it different from CoolSculpting or liposuction?

CoolSculpting freezes fat and can leave weeks of numbness, swelling, or tenderness; liposuction is surgery. Zerona is gentler and its results are more modest, but it involves no pain, no recovery, and no risk of contour irregularities. It suits people who want a measurable change without any downtime.

Who is it for?

Adults who are close to a healthy weight with stubborn areas that diet and exercise have not shifted. It works best in the BMI range the device was studied in (roughly 25 to 40) and as a complement to a weight or wellness program, not as a substitute for one. It is not used during pregnancy, and suitability is confirmed at your consultation.

How much does Zerona cost?

Sessions are $250 each, or $200 each when you buy a package of four ($800). A full course is six sessions, so most people combine a four-pack with two additional sessions. Your consultation is free, and pricing is confirmed before you start.
