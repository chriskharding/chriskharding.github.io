Peptide Therapy · Orlando · Educational

Your body already knows how to heal.
Peptides are how it signals.

Peptides are short chains of amino acids — naturally occurring signaling molecules that tell cells to repair, regenerate, and perform. As we age, those signals weaken. This page is a plain-English primer on what peptides are and how they’re used at HMI — as part of a comprehensive, lab-guided, provider-supervised plan. It’s educational information, not medical advice.

A primer

Peptides discussed in concierge medicine.

The list below is educational — it describes what each peptide has been studied for. It is not a recommendation, a menu, or a promise of results. What (if anything) belongs in your plan is decided with a provider, based on your labs and history.

Growth-hormone support

CJC-1295 + Ipamorelin

A widely used peptide combination studied for its role in prompting the pituitary to release growth hormone. Discussed in the context of body composition, sleep quality, and recovery.

Discussed for: body recomposition, sleep, recovery

Tissue repair & recovery

BPC-157 (± TB-500)

Body Protection Compound — among the more extensively studied peptides in the recovery space. Researched for its role in the repair of connective tissue and the gut lining; sometimes paired with TB-500.

Discussed for: injury recovery, joints, gut health

GH secretagogue

Sermorelin

One of the longer-studied peptides in this category — a growth-hormone-releasing-hormone analog researched for gradually supporting the body’s own GH production.

Discussed for: healthy aging, GH decline

Cellular energy & longevity

NAD+

A coenzyme involved in cellular energy metabolism. Levels decline with age; injectable NAD+ is often discussed as an at-home complement to in-clinic IV therapy.

Discussed for: energy, cognitive performance, longevity

Skin, hair & regeneration

GHK-Cu / GLOW blend

A copper-peptide complex studied for its effects on skin architecture, hair-follicle density, and connective tissue — a natural complement to our aesthetics services.

Discussed for: skin quality, hair, post-procedure recovery

Body composition

Tesamorelin

A GHRH analog and the one peptide in this group with an FDA approval (for a specific indication), studied in the context of visceral (abdominal) fat and metabolic markers.

Discussed for: visceral fat, metabolic health
