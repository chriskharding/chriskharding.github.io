Our story

Founded on a simple belief: patients deserve
a doctor's full attention.

Dr. Deborah Harding · Founder

Harding Medical Institute was founded in Orlando by Dr. Deborah Harding and Dr. Victor Harding with a conviction that ran against the grain of modern medicine: that healthcare works best when a clinician truly knows the patient in front of them.

Dr. Deborah Harding built HMI into one of Central Florida's most trusted destinations for hormone optimization and preventive wellness — a practice where visits were unhurried, labs were read line by line, and patients were treated like family, because many of them became exactly that.

Today, HMI remains family-owned and carries that legacy forward. The medicine has advanced — comprehensive panels calibrated to the HMI Standard, precision supplementation, modern hormone therapies — but the philosophy hasn't moved an inch. Every member of our team is here because they believe what Dr. Deborah Harding believed:

“Attention is the most powerful instrument in medicine.”
