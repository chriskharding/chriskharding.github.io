Common questions

Windermere patients ask us.

Do you have an office in Windermere?

No — we have one office, in MetroWest at 6200 Metrowest Blvd, Suite 105, Orlando. From Windermere it is about 15 minutes by car, and many patients use telehealth for follow-ups where clinically appropriate.

How soon can I be seen?

We offer same-week appointments for new patients. Call (407) 210-2101 or use the form on this page, and our team calls back within 15 minutes during business hours.

Do you take insurance?

We are a direct-pay practice and provide a superbill for possible out-of-network reimbursement. Labs are billed to your insurance where possible, and HSA/FSA funds are often accepted. We have opted out of Medicare.

What makes HMI different from a walk-in clinic near me?

Time and depth. Visits are unhurried, labs are read against optimal ranges rather than "normal", and one provider follows your plan over time. It is concierge-style care at a single location, built for people who want answers rather than a prescription and a wave.
