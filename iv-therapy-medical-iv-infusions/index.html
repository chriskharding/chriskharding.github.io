Inside every infusion

What’s in the bag, and why it’s there.

Revive Hydration & hangover · Tier 1

In the bag: Prescription anti-nausea medication (Rx) · Vita-Complex (B1, B2, B3, B5, B6) · mineral blend · 1,000 mL normal saline

Why it’s there: The fast reset for dehydration, travel fatigue, and the morning after. Prescription anti-nausea medication settles the stomach while a full liter of fluids and B vitamins restore what was depleted first.

Restore Pain & muscle recovery · Tier 2

In the bag: Calcium chloride · magnesium chloride · hydroxocobalamin (B12) · Vita-Complex · optional prescription anti-inflammatory (Rx)

Why it’s there: For aching muscles, tension, and the physical toll of chronic strain. Calcium and magnesium are the minerals of muscle contraction and release; a prescription anti-inflammatory may be added at your provider’s discretion.

Reset Weight & metabolic support · Tier 2

In the bag: Taurine · methylcobalamin (B12) · Vita-Complex

Why it’s there: Built to complement a medically supervised weight program. Supports fat metabolism and energy production — and covers the B-vitamin demands that rise on an active plan or GLP-1 protocol.

Recover Immune boost · Tier 2

In the bag: Preserved ascorbic acid 500 mg/mL · zinc sulfate · Vita-Complex

Why it’s there: For the first hint of a rough week — or the week before travel, surgery, or a season of long days. High-dose vitamin C and zinc, the two nutrients most studied for immune defense, at levels oral dosing cannot reach.

Renew Inner beauty · Tier 3

In the bag: Preserved ascorbic acid 500 mg/mL · biotin · Vita-Complex

Why it’s there: Beauty from the inside out. High-dose vitamin C is a required cofactor for collagen formation; biotin supports the keratin structures behind strong hair and nails.

Reignite Circulation & libido · Tier 3

In the bag: Arginine 100 mg/mL · niacinamide (B3) · pyridoxine (B6) · taurine

Why it’s there: An arginine-forward formulation. Arginine is the body’s precursor to nitric oxide — the molecule that relaxes and opens blood vessels — supporting healthy circulation, performance, and vitality.

Recharge NAD+ 250 mg · Advanced

In the bag: NAD+ 250 mg in 500 mL normal saline, infused slowly for comfort · 500 mg, 750 mg, and 1,000 mg protocols by consultation

Why it’s there: NAD+ is the coenzyme every cell uses to turn fuel into energy — and levels decline steadily with age. A two-hour infusion supporting cellular energy, mental clarity, and healthy aging.

The building blocks. Vita-Complex: thiamine B1 · riboflavin B2 · niacinamide B3 · dexpanthenol B5 · pyridoxine B6 — the full energy-metabolism family. Mineral blend: magnesium · zinc · manganese · copper. All infusions are built in 500–1,000 mL of normal saline and compounded by an FDA-registered 503B outsourcing facility. Formulations shown are standard preparations; your clinician may adjust doses and components to your labs, medications, and goals.

Common questions

IV therapy questions, answered.

How much does IV therapy cost in Orlando?

Revitalize with Rowe infusions come in three simple tiers: Tier 1 is $175 ($145 for Infusion Club members), Tier 2 is $235 ($195 members), and Tier 3 is $285 ($235 members). NAD+ 250 mg is $400 ($350 members), with 500 mg, 750 mg, and 1,000 mg protocols available by consultation. Any infusion is 10% off as a series of three. Ten-minute booster shots run $35 to $95. Call (407) 210-2101 for same-week availability.

How does the Infusion Club work?

Three levels, no contract: Essential is $149/month for one Tier 1 infusion monthly and 15% off extras; Signature is $219/month for one infusion of any tier and 20% off; Elite is $429/month for two infusions of any tier — or NAD+ plus a Tier 1 — with 25% off and member pricing extended to your household. Every level unlocks the member price on every infusion plus priority scheduling. Cancel anytime.

What is Revitalize with Rowe?

Revitalize with Rowe is Harding Medical Institute’s IV nutrient therapy program, led by Rowena Montojo-Weiss, APRN. Every infusion on the menu is formulated, reviewed, and administered by HMI’s clinical team, integrated with your hormone, peptide, and metabolic care rather than sold as a stand-alone drip.

Which infusion should I choose?

Start with how you feel: dehydrated or rough (Revive), sore and strained (Restore), on a weight or metabolic plan (Reset), fighting something off or preparing for surgery (Recover), focused on skin, hair, and healthy aging (Renew), circulation and vitality (Reignite), or cellular energy and longevity (Recharge NAD+). If you’re not sure, our team matches an infusion to your labs, symptoms, and goals — that’s the whole point of a clinician-led menu.

How is IV therapy different from taking vitamins?

Oral supplements pass through a digestive system that absorbs only a fraction of what you swallow. Intravenous delivery places vitamins, minerals, and amino acids straight into circulation, at concentrations the gut cannot reach. Many patients report feeling replenished the same day.

Who administers the infusions, and is it safe?

Every infusion is administered by licensed clinical staff following a provider evaluation. Items marked Rx contain prescription medication and require a good-faith examination and provider order. Please disclose all medications, supplements, allergies, and any history of kidney disease, heart failure, or G6PD deficiency. IV nutrient therapy is a wellness service intended to supplement — not replace — medical care.

What is precision supplementation?

Instead of guessing at the vitamin aisle, we run comprehensive labs, read your numbers against the HMI Standard — optimal ranges, not “normal” ones — and formulate a single personalized, pharmaceutical-grade daily pill that covers exactly what your body needs. It has its own program page at hardingmedicalinstitute.com/precision-supplementation/.

Do I need an appointment?

We offer same-week appointments for infusions and booster shots. NAD+ requires a consultation first. Call (407) 210-2101 or book online, and our team will confirm your time — usually within one business day.
