Common questions

IV therapy questions, answered.

How much does IV therapy cost in Orlando?

Our six drips run $179 to $299: Quench $179, Get Up and Go $199, Immunity $219, Reboot $249, and Snow Bright (glutathione) $299. NAD+ starts at $499 with personalized dosing. Members pay about 15% less on every drip — the Monthly Optimal membership is $159/month and includes a monthly drip. First-timers can start with The Reset at $159. Call (407) 210-2101 for same-week availability.

How does the IV membership work?

Monthly Optimal is $159/month: you get one standard drip every month (any of the five, up to a $299 value), member pricing of roughly 15% off every additional drip — NAD+ included — and unused drips roll over, capped at two banked at any time. It’s shareable with a family member and you can cancel anytime. If you drip more than once a quarter, membership wins.

Which IV drip should I choose?

Start with how you feel: dehydrated or dragging (Quench), low energy (Get Up and Go), fighting something off (Immunity), feeling rough after illness or a long weekend (Reboot), focused on skin and healthy aging (Snow Bright with glutathione), or investing in longevity (NAD+). If you’re not sure, our team will match a drip to how you’re feeling — that’s what we’re here for.

How is IV therapy different from taking vitamins?

IV infusion delivers fluids and nutrients directly into the bloodstream, bypassing digestion — so absorption isn’t limited by what your gut can process. Many patients report feeling replenished the same day.

What is precision supplementation?

Instead of guessing at the vitamin aisle, we run comprehensive labs, read your numbers against the HMI Standard — optimal ranges, not “normal” ones — and formulate a single personalized, pharmaceutical-grade daily pill that covers exactly what your body needs. It has its own program page at hardingmedicalinstitute.com/precision-supplementation/, and the $449 HMI Standard Bundle is the easiest way to start.

Do I need an appointment?

We offer same-week appointments for IV therapy and lab draws. Call (407) 210-2101 or book online, and our team will confirm your time — usually within one business day.
